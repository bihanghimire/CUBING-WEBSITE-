F2L (FIRST TWO LAYERS)

F2L, or the First Two Layers, is a crucial step in solving a Rubik's Cube. It involves solving the corner and edge pieces simultaneously into their correct positions

ALGORTHMS OF F2L

CASE 1

ALGORITHM : U R U'R'

TIPS:A fully connected pair. Align with U, then slide it straight into the front-right slot.

CASE 2

ALGORITHM: F R' F' R

TIPS: A nearly assembled pair. Only the edge orientation is off—fix it with U, then place it into the front-right slot.

CASE 3

ALGORITHM: F' U F

TIPS: Edge on the left, corner on URF. Use F to bring them together, form the pair, and drop it into the slot.

CASE 4

ALGORITHM: R U R'

TIPS: The corner is already near its correct spot while the edge rests on the front. Line them up, pair them, and place them into the front-right slot.

CASE 5

ALGORITHM: U' R U R' U2 R U' R'

TIPS: The corner is twisted and the edge is in the back. Move the corner away first, then handle it like a regular pairing case.

CASE 6

ALGORITHM: U F' U' F U2' F' U F

TIPS: Bring the yellow sticker to front-left with U, hide the corner with F, shift the edge with U’, then restore with F to create a clean pairing position.

CASE 7

ALGORITHM: U' R U2 R' U' R U2 R'

TIPS: Move the corner from front-right to back-right, keeping yellow outward. Reposition the edge, bring the corner back, and finish the slot-in

CASE 8

ALGORITHM: U F' U2' F U2' F' U F

TIPS: Rotate the corner so yellow faces front-left. Hide it with F’, bring the edge beside it, then restore with F to form the pair.

CASE 9

ALGORITHM: U' R U' R' U F' U' F

TIPS: Turn the corner’s yellow to the back-right, drop it with R, bring the edge forward, restore, then fold with F' to pair them.

CASE 10

ALGORITHM: U F' U F U' R U' R'

TIPS:Move the corner’s yellow to the front-left. Separate them with F’, reposition the edge to the opposite side, then fold them together

CASE 11

ALGORITHM: U' R U2 R' U F' U' F

TIPS: The pieces are touching but mismatched. Separate them first, then place the edge where you can pair easil

CASE 12

ALGORITHM: R U' R' U R U' R' U2 R U' R'

TIPS: Another mismatched-but-touching case. Move the corner away, slide the edge into place, then fold them together.

CASE 13

ALGORITHM: U F' U F U' F' U' F

TIPS: Corner and edge are touching but misaligned. Hide the corner with F’, move the edge, then fold them together at the right moment.

CASE 14

ALGORITHM: U' R U' R' U R U R'

TIPS: Corner is twisted on top, edge buried in the right layer. Extract the edge first, then form the pair.

CASE 15

ALGORITHM: F' U F U2 R U R'

TIPS: Corner and edge are attached but don't match—split them apart first, then everything becomes simple.

CASE 16

ALGORITHM: R U' R' U2 F' U' F

TIPS : They pass by each other—separate them first. Move the corner away, slide the edge, restore, then fold them together once yellow is in front-right.

CASE 17

ALGORITHM: R U2 R' U' R U R'

TIPS : Corner faces upward and the colors don't match. Move the corner away with R, shift with U2, reposition, and fold them together for a clean slot-in.

CASE 18

ALGORITHM: F' U2 F U F' U' F

TIPS: Corner is twisted up and the front edge doesn't match. Hide with F’, separate, restore, bring yellow to front-right, then fold them together

CASE 19

ALGORITHM: U R U2 R2 F R F'

TIPS: Corner is ready but the edge is too far. Bring yellow to upper-left, free the edge, then move the corner toward it for a smooth slot-in.

CASE 20

ALGORITHM: U' F' U2 F U' F' U F

TIPS: Move the corner to back-right, extract the edge with F’, bring them together, and finish the slot-i

CASE 21

ALGORITHM: U2 R U R' U R U' R'

TIPS: Move the corner to back-left, shift the edge to back-right, bring them together, and drop them into the slot.

CASE 22

ALGORITHM: F' L' U2 L F

TIPS: When the corner is on top and the edge matches the front color, align the front and use F' to free the edge. Move the corner into position and they instantly pair. Then insert.

CASE 23

ALGORITHM: U R U' R' U' R U' R' U R U' R'

TIPS: Swap the corner and edge positions. Drop the corner into the right-bottom slot, bring the edge to the top-right, let them meet in the back-right position, then insert the pair.

CASE 24

ALGORITHM: F U R U' R' F' R U' R'

TIPS: Reverse the order of the corner and edge. Use F to move the corner away, slide the edge over, restore the corner, and the pair forms. Insert immediately.

CASE 25

ALGORITHM: R' F' R U R U' R' F

TIPS: The corner is already inserted. Match the edge with the front color and insert with a simple left-side setup.

CASE 26

ALGORITHM: U R U' R' F R' F R

TIPS: The corner is already in place. Rotate the right side to position the edge and insert smoothly.

CASE 27

ALGORITHM: R U' R' U R U' R'

TIPS: The corner is inserted but oriented incorrectly. Bring it out to the top—sometimes the pair forms immediately. Insert once they match

CASE 28

ALGORITHM: R U R' U' F R' F R

TIPS: The corner is inserted but misoriented. Pull it out to the top—often the pair aligns right away. Then insert cleanly

CASE 29

ALGORITHM: R' F R F' U R U' R'

TIPS: The corner is inserted but misoriented, and bringing it to the top won’t pair it. Move it to the back through the bottom using an F move to set up a workable position.

CASE 30

ALGORITHM: R U R' U' R U R'

TIPS: The corner is inserted but oriented wrong. Bring it out to the top, then rotate it to the far side and insert the pair smoothly.

CASE 31

ALGORITHM: U' R' F R F' R U' R'

TIPS: The pieces are almost paired. Bring them out to the top layer first, then insert for an easy solution.

CASE 32

ALGORITHM: U R U' R' U R U' R' U R U' R'

TIPS: A lucky case — a quick R move and the pair falls right into the slot.

CASE 33

ALGORITHM: U' R U' R' U2 R U' R'

TIPS: Just move the corner to the back-right, align with the edge, and drop it into the slot.

CASE 34

ALGORITHM: U R U R' U2 R U R'

TIPS:They’re twisted apart. Separate with U R, position corner front-right and edge in the back, then R to pair and slot them in.

CASE 35

ALGORITHM: U' R U R' U F' U' F

TIPS: Bring the corner to the back-right with U', lift the edge out, set the corner front-right, and use F' to form the pair.

CASE 36

ALGORITHM: U F' U' F U' R U R'

TIPS: The corner and edge are twisted together. Use U F' to separate them, then the pair forms instantly and can be inserted normally

CASE 37

ALGORITHM: R2 U2 F R2 F' U2 R' U R'

TIPS: Both pieces are in the slot but mismatched. Bring the corner out, separate them, and reposition. After the reset, they naturally form a clean pair ready for insertion.

CASE 38

ALGORITHM: R U' R' U' R U R' U2 R U' R'

TIPS: Separate the pair with R U'. Move the corner to the back-right and bring the edge closer—this sets up an easy, clean insertion.

CASE 39

ALGORITHM: R U' R' U R U2 R' U R U' R'

TIPS: The pieces are stuck together but misaligned. Separate them, tuck the edge into the back-right, then bring the corner over to form the pair

CASE 40

ALGORITHM: R F U R U' R' F' U' R'

TIPS: Separate the pieces, face them toward each other, then use R to pair and insert smoothly.

CASE 41

ALGORITHM: R U F R U R' U' F' R'

TIPS: A tangled case. Use F to swap the corner and edge positions—this fixes their order and creates a clean pair for insertion.

THESE ARE ALL THE ALGORITHMS OF F2L .
